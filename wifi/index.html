Multi-Link Operation (MLO) STR Mode

Allows devices to send and receive data simultaneously across multiple frequency bands (2.4 GHz, 5 GHz, 6 GHz), bypassing interference in real time.

4096-QAM Modulation

Encodes 12 bits per symbol (compared to 10 bits in 1024-QAM), yielding a 20% increase in peak data rates.

Preamble Puncturing

Rather than dropping an entire 160/320 MHz channel due to localized interference, Wi-Fi 7 "punctures" only the affected sub-channel while keeping the rest active.

Uses multi-antenna arrays to transmit distinct beamformed spatial streams to separate devices at the exact same frequency.
